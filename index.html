How the course works

One predictable routine for every two-week module

Use the approved Bread Board plan and the same four-step learning routine in every module.

1. Read and inspect

Study three substantial theory sections and refer to the approved Bread Board plan.

2. Practise

Answer multiple-choice questions. Incorrect choices provide a useful clue and another attempt.

3. Apply

Write scaffolded responses, review live concept guidance and compare with a model after a genuine attempt.

4. Review and save

Autosave in this browser, then use Print / Save PDF to keep evidence outside the webpage.

Course map

Five modules covering the complete ten-week project

Weeks 1–2: Readiness, risk and accurate marking

Interpret the project brief, prepare the shared workshop, manage changing hazards and mark from a reliable datum.

Open Module 1

Weeks 3–4: Layout, biscuits and controlled shaping

Read grain direction, organise component orientation, align biscuit-joint positions and preserve the planned form while shaping.

Open Module 2

Weeks 5–6: Dry assembly, glue-up and routed profiles

Check the complete assembly before joining, plan balanced clamping and prepare safely for a teacher-directed routed profile.

Open Module 3

Weeks 7–8: Surface preparation and responsible finishing

Inspect and sand systematically, prepare for the approved oil finish and manage materials, contamination and waste responsibly.

Open Module 4

Weeks 9–10: Evaluation, folio and workshop handover

Judge the completed project against evidence, select authentic folio records and leave the shared workshop ready for others.
