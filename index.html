How the course works

One predictable routine for every two-week module

Read the project theory first, use feedback to correct misunderstandings, then produce written evidence that explains your practical decisions.

1. Read and inspect

Study three substantial theory sections and refer to the approved Bread Board plan.

2. Practise

Answer multiple-choice questions. Incorrect choices provide a useful clue and another attempt.

3. Apply

Write scaffolded responses, review live concept guidance and compare with a model after a genuine attempt.

4. Review and save

Autosave in this browser, then use Print / Save PDF to keep evidence outside the webpage.

Save your work: Your work is saved in this browser on this device. When you finish a module, use Print / Save PDF. Upload the PDF to Google Classroom when your teacher tells you to.

Project stages

From safe preparation to honest evaluation

  1. PrepareRead the brief, identify risk and establish accurate marking references.
  2. FormOrganise grain, joint positions and shaping boundaries from the plan.
  3. AssembleDry fit, diagnose alignment and plan controlled glue-up.
  4. FinishPrepare a consistent surface and manage finish materials responsibly.
  5. EvaluateUse evidence to judge quality, document learning and hand over the workshop.

Syllabus outcomes

Visible Stage 5 Industrial Technology connections

The Bread Board project retains the original program's mapping across safe work, design, production, communication, evaluation and responsible use of technology.

  1. IND5-1: identifies, assesses, applies and manages WHS risks associated with tools, equipment, materials, processes and technologies.
  2. IND5-2: applies design principles in the modification, development and production of projects.
  3. IND5-3: selects and uses appropriate hand and machine tools, equipment and processes to produce quality practical projects.
  4. IND5-4: selects, justifies and uses relevant materials for specific applications.
  5. IND5-5: interprets and applies suitable communication techniques in planning, production and presentation.
  6. IND5-6: participates in collaborative work practices in the learning environment.
  7. IND5-7: applies and transfers skills, processes and materials to different contexts and projects.
  8. IND5-8: evaluates products for functional, economic, aesthetic and environmental qualities and quality of construction.
  9. IND5-9: describes, analyses and uses current, new and emerging technologies and their applications.
  10. IND5-10: describes, analyses and evaluates impacts of technology on society, the environment and cultural issues.
