# Breadboard Guided Course rebuild plan

The legacy `Yr-9-Breadboard` site remains read-only. This separate project reuses its scope, plans and project image, but does not reuse its layout or student-facing theory.

## Course map

| Module | Weeks | Practical focus | Source coverage retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1–2 | Workshop readiness, the brief, marking, hand tools and timber selection | Safety, signs, measuring, planing and timber defects |
| 2 | 3–4 | Board layout, biscuit joints, cutting ends and shaping | Grain direction, biscuit joiner, jigsaw, disc sander and design choices |
| 3 | 5–6 | Dry assembly, glue-up, clamping and routed profiles | Adhesives, clamping, laser work and router safety |
| 4 | 7–8 | Sanding, surface preparation, oil finishing and sustainability | Abrasives, timber seasoning, finishes, SWMS and environmental responsibility |
| 5 | 9–10 | Evaluation, presentation and workshop care | Self-evaluation, documentation, maintenance and project presentation |

## Required student experience

Every module will contain three ChatGPT-authored theory sections, only purposeful imagery, knowledge checks with feedback, scaffolded written responses with feedback, student details, autosave and Print / Save PDF evidence. The course landing page will include the original plans, course map, outcomes, resources and a clear link to a twelve-card evidence folio.

## Publication rule

The new project will publish as `breadboard-guided-course`; `Yr-9-Breadboard` will not be modified.
